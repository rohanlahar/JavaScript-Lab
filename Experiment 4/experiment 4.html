🚗 Vehicle Registration Validation

Validation Rules

  • Vehicle Name should not be empty.
  • Registration Number should not be empty.
  • Registration Number must contain exactly 10 characters.
  • First 2 characters must be uppercase alphabets (State Code).
  • Next 2 characters must be digits (District Code).
  • Next 2 characters must be uppercase alphabets (Series).
  • Last 4 characters must be digits (Vehicle Number).
